About RaceMind

Training that fits a full life.

RaceMind grew out of a triathlon build of its own, made by someone who has to fit family, a company and training into the same week.

Why RaceMind exists

I wanted to prepare for Challenge Roth 2026, and none of the existing apps really fit. So I started tinkering on my own, first with ChatGPT and Notion. When more and more people asked about the system, the side project became an app of its own.

As a father of two and an entrepreneur, I know how hard it is to fit structured training into a busy life. That’s exactly what RaceMind is for.

Dominik WinterFounder of RaceMind

What we stick to

What RaceMind was meant to be from day one.

Build up, don’t jump

Structured endurance training without jumps in load. Not straight from 10 km to the marathon.

Rules, not gut feeling

The plan comes from training logic you can check the math on, not from a model that makes things up.

Your life first

Training adapts to your life, not the other way round.
