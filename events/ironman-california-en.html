IRONMAN California

October 18, 2026 Sacramento, USA
← Back to calendar

Course description

Extremely fast PR course. Current-assisted downriver swim in the Sacramento River, pancake-flat bike and run.

Coach tip

The downriver swim is fast — bank the time, but do not overcook the flat bike.
