September 6, 2026 Köln, Deutschland
← Back to calendar

Course description

Middle distance in the heart of Cologne: a 2.6 km current-assisted Rhine swim, 87.3 km on fully closed roads across the Rhine bridges out to Lake Fühlingen and back, and a 19 km run finishing at the Tanzbrunnen.

Coach tip

The Rhine swim is longer than usual but faster thanks to the current – sight the exit early.

Closed roads tempt you to overbike, stick to your power target.

The riverside run is flat and offers hardly any shade.
