Ironman Lanzarote

May 15, 2027 Puerto del Carmen, Spanien
← Back to calendar

Course description

One of the toughest Ironman races in the world. The swim is in the Atlantic at Puerto del Carmen (two laps with an Australian exit), followed by a 180 km bike with around 2,500 metres of climbing around the volcanic island. Fierce trade winds and lava fields make for a brutal day.

Coach tip

A disc wheel is allowed but may be banned at short notice in storms — in crosswinds the front wheel is the bigger problem anyway. The climbs aren't steep but are long and wind-battered: ride to power. Here, mental toughness matters more than any pacing target.
