Course description

A flat half distance on the Pacific: an open-water swim, a fast bike course with no notable climbs and a flat run along the Panama City skyline. Fast on paper — the humidity turns it into a different race.

Coach tip

Flat does not mean fast here: in high humidity sweat barely cools you, so plan the run a level slower than your best on a flat course.

Test your fluid and salt strategy somewhere warm beforehand, not in European February training.

The bike course tempts you to go all-out because nothing slows you down — that is exactly what breaks the run.
