Course description

A flat, fast season finale in the historic fishing town of Cervia. The swim is in the Adriatic Sea, followed by a two-loop bike through the salt-pans nature park (home to flamingos), with only a short climb near Bertinoro. The flat run and pasta party make this race a favourite.

Coach tip

Flat and fast, except for the short climb to Bertinoro — ride it under control and stay aero otherwise. In September jellyfish can appear in the sea; sight the buoys actively on the swim. Use the flat run to hold an even PB pace and take on fuel regularly throughout.
