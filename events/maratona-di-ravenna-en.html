Ravenna Marathon

November 8, 2026 Ravenna, Italien
← Back to calendar

Course description

A flat autumn marathon in Romagna, past the Byzantine mosaics and basilicas of the old town. Adriatic November temperatures and a course with no notable climbing.

Coach tip

A flat course in November is the best combination for a personal best — the heat is gone and so is the profile.

The date sits late in the season: build so that your peak does not already fall in September.

The old-town sections are narrow and twisty; do not let the field dictate your first kilometres.
