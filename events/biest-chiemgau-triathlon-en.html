BIEST Chiemgau Triathlon

June 19, 2027 Chieming, Deutschland
← Back to calendar

Course description

A premiere on Lake Chiemsee: in 2027 TSV Chieming hosts the first BIEST, a long distance in its own format with a 3 km swim, 120 km bike and 30 km run. The swim is a single loop in Lake Chiemsee, from the start in Chieming out towards Fraueninsel. The 40 km bike loop with about 300 m of climbing runs on closed roads through the Chiemgau and is ridden three times. The run is flat along the lake shore to just before Arlaching and back, three 10 km laps. Racing starts from 1 pm, and the finish closes at 11 pm.

Coach tip

The three 40 km bike laps add up to about 900 m of climbing. Take the first lap deliberately easy, the course repeats twice more. Overtaking is strictly forbidden in the underpasses and on the short stretch of bike path. Aid stations are at km 10, 30, 50, 70, 90 and 110, with water, sports drink, gels, bars and bananas.

The 1 pm start pushes the whole race day back. Race packs are available on Friday from 3 to 8 pm in the marquee at the Markstatt, and check-in is on Saturday from 8 to 11:30 am. Try some long sessions in the afternoon and plan your eating for the late start. Cut-offs: swim 3 pm, bike 7 pm, finish 11 pm.

The run is a flat 10 km out-and-back course along the shore, which you run three times. There are four aid stations per lap, at km 1, 3.5, 6 and 8.5, with cola and Red Bull on the run course too. The referees decide one hour before the start whether wetsuits are allowed. The entry fee is not refunded if you do not start.
