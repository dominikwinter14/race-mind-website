Course description

The "BarockMan" full distance at the Schloss-Triathlon Moritzburg near Dresden, in its 25th edition in 2027. The swim is two anticlockwise laps of the castle pond around the castle island. The closed, gently rolling 28.75 km bike loop with 166 m of climbing is ridden six times, 173 km in total instead of 180. The marathon covers six 7 km laps around the castle pond and through the Moritzburg forest, on tarmac, firm paths and forest trail. With just over 100 finishers in 2026, a small long-distance race.

Coach tip

Six bike laps of 166 m each add up to around 1000 m of climbing. Ride the short rollers with a power cap from the start — the last two laps decide how you start the marathon.

Cut-offs in 2026: swim 2:30 h, onto the run within 10 hours of the start, finish closes at 10 pm. The roads are closed, but vehicles are still possible — keep right.

The marathon is six 7 km laps, mostly shaded in the forest. Split it into laps and fuel on every one — each lap has at least two aid stations and a water point.
