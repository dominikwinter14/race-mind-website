December 6, 2026 Busselton, Australien
← Back to calendar

Course description

One of the fastest courses anywhere. Calm, clear swim by the Busselton Jetty, pancake-flat bike and run.

Coach tip

The course is ideal for a personal best — stay aerodynamic in the sea breeze and run your target pace from the start.
