VR Bank Triathlon Heilbronn Middle Distance

June 13, 2027 Heilbronn, Deutschland
← Back to calendar

Course description

A middle distance over 1.9 / 83 / 21.5 km in the heart of Heilbronn. The swim is an out-and-back course in the Neckar river, with a rolling start at the Obere Neckarstraße. The bike course covers two laps through the Heilbronner Land and the Zabergäu, with 1,100 m of climbing and demanding ascents according to the organiser. The run is four flat, shaded laps along the Neckar, finishing on the market square in the old town.

Coach tip

In the Neckar you swim out to the turn buoy at km 1.0 and back. Rolling start at 8:15 am, three athletes every 5 seconds — line up honestly by your swim time. The referees decide on wetsuits one hour before the start; in 2026 the Neckar was around 19.5°C. The swim cut-off is 1:10 h.

Two laps with a combined 1,100 m of climbing, which the organiser calls demanding: train long climbs and fast descents, not just flat kilometres, and pace the first lap. Drafting is not allowed. You must finish the swim and bike within 5:30 h.

Four flat run laps with turnarounds along the Neckar to the market square, overall cut-off 8:00 h. Bike check-in is Saturday 2–7 pm or race morning 6:00–7:45 am; race packets on race morning need prior registration by email, so collect yours beforehand. Without a race licence you pay a €25 day licence on top; registration closes on 2 June 2027, and in 2026 it sold out weeks ahead.
