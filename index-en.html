01 Why am I not getting faster?

“I train – but I’m not moving forward.”

You train regularly. Yet your times stagnate. The most common reason: training without clear structure – too often medium-hard, too rarely truly easy, too rarely deliberately intense. That costs progress – or it ends in injury.

  • Periodized plan – alternating load and recovery, tuned to your fitness level.
  • Plain language after every session – what it did, what matters next time.
  • You understand your training – the physiology behind it, not blindly ticking off zones.
  • Readiness check every morning – “Push today” or “Take it easy,” instead of HRV charts.

02 You’re signed up. Now what?

“The start spot is yours – the plan isn’t.”

Between signing up and the start line lie many weeks – and even more questions. How much training is enough? What’s too much? And is the goal even realistic?

  • Race time prediction from day 1 – more accurate every week.
  • A plan that fits your time – no 15-hour plan when you have 7.
  • Safely to the finish – the engine deliberately holds back, guarding against too much, too soon.
  • Race day included – pacing and nutrition plan for race day.

03 A coach would be great. But €200 a month?

“Between a PDF plan and a premium coach.”

A personal coach isn’t an option for most – too expensive, too much coordination. A PDF plan is the other extreme: cheap, but no one who answers. RaceMind makes professional training planning accessible – from €6.66 a month, cancel anytime.

  • Ask whatever is on your mind – “It’s meant to rain on race day – what does that mean for my pacing?” “Why was my long run so hard?” “How many gels do I need?”
  • A coach that answers – around the clock, with your data and your plan in context.
  • A coach that acts – “Move my intervals to Thursday” – done.

04 Cycle Tracking

Your coach doesn’t ignore your cycle.

Most training plans act as if your cycle didn’t exist. RaceMind listens: when you enable cycle tracking, your cycle feeds into load and planning as context – for example when perceived exertion rises in the luteal phase and recovery matters more. Optional – you decide whether and how, and can turn it off anytime.

  • Carry on as normal – if you don’t want adjustments, nothing changes in your plan.
  • More cautious in the luteal phase – the engine deliberately takes load out before your period.
  • Protect period days – hard sessions step aside; you make up the load later.

06 And when a week falls apart?

Your life isn’t linear – why should your training be?

RaceMind replans your week – around the time you have now. The coach explains what stays, what drops, and why that’s okay. The trigger is interchangeable: a deadline, bad sleep, a cold – or your period. All of it is normal.

  • Built for weeks that don’t go to plan. RaceMind puts your training calendar back in order – without rescheduling, without catching up.
  • Keep going instead of quitting. You keep training – without guilt and without chaos in your head.
  • Back after illness or injury. A progressive way back instead of starting over – conservative enough to avoid setbacks.

Frequently Asked Questions

“I’ve been training for a long time – is RaceMind only for beginners?”

No. Especially when your times stagnate, RaceMind helps bring structure and periodization to your training. Your existing data from Garmin, COROS, Strava, or intervals.icu isn’t just displayed – it’s translated into a plan, a race time prediction, and concrete decisions for your week.

“What happens if I can’t train at all for a week?”

You don’t have to make up for anything. RaceMind can shift, adjust, or skip sessions without your week falling apart. When a single tweak isn’t enough, the week gets replanned instead of just pushing everything back.

“How does RaceMind protect me from overload and injury?”

RaceMind doesn’t just assess what you could handle – it also considers what makes sense right now. For beginners especially, the engine deliberately holds back – guarding you against too much, too soon. Sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and training load all factor in. That said: RaceMind is a training coach, not a substitute for medical or physiotherapy advice.

“How does cycle tracking work – and do I have to use it?”

You don’t have to. If you enable it, RaceMind can factor your cycle into load and planning. Depending on your preference, the plan can continue normally during that time, become slightly more cautious, or protect period days more.

“What sets you apart from TrainingPeaks/TriDot/Humango?”

RaceMind combines three things that are often separate: adaptation, explainability, and direct action. Instead of just showing data, the coach explains what it means. Instead of just giving recommendations, RaceMind can move sessions or replan your week directly.

“Can I cancel or pause my plan if I stop training?”

Yes, you can cancel anytime. Cancellation is handled through the subscription management in the App Store or Google Play. And if you want to delete your account entirely, that’s possible too.
